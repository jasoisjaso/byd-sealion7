### Cross-check (second independent read-out of the same five images, 2026-10-04)
- A second reader transcribed the same images and agreed on every label, the finger position in shot2 (left-side text, not the blue Reset link) and the hidden-page button order.
- Both readers: all five images are landscape; none shows the hidden page in portrait, and none shows an "adb is on" message.
- Provenance for the checklist steps the screenshots do NOT show: the portrait rotation (step 3) and the "adb is on" confirmation message (step 4) come from the BYD Lab guide, bydlab.dev/guides/sealion-7-adb-wifi-install-app/, tested on head-unit build 51.1.4.2606220.1. shot2 shows build 51.1.4.26060... on the German car, so the two builds are near-identical.
- What the BYD Lab guide states (re-read 2026-10-04, page marked "Verified 26 Aug 2026"): Moroccan-market Sealion 7, Settings shows V3.1.0, Android 11, arm64-v8a, ro.adb.secure=1, no Play Services. Step 2: tap the words "Réinitialisation d'usine" (Factory reset) on the left of the last row "seven times at a steady pace, roughly two taps per second"; "Nothing on the screen acknowledges the taps until the sequence is complete; if it did nothing, wait a second and try again at a more even rhythm." Step 3: "Rotate the display to portrait with the rotate button at the bottom right of the bar." Step 4: tap the first button; "A message confirms that adb is on." Step 8: "adb disconnect" only closes the computer's session, "the car keeps listening on 5555"; whether the switch turns itself off when the head unit restarts is listed as not tested, as is how to turn it off other than by restarting. The guide's own limitations: one car, one market, one build; the hidden page's labels were not recorded (the author navigated by position). The checklist's tap cadence, "nothing until the 7th tap", rotate-button position and V3.1.0/Morocco attribution all come from this guide.
- The landscape shots do show the long button labels running off the left edge (shot7), which is consistent with the guide's reason for rotating to portrait.
- The BYD Lab guide also states the unit enforces adb authentication (ro.adb.secure=1), so the "Allow USB debugging?" dialog on the car screen is expected for a new computer; the German reviewer's Buddy-Load session did not show one, which is why the checklist says "if it appears".
